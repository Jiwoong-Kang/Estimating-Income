### 2. What logistic regression is

- Like a linear model (weighted sum of features), but squeezes the output to a **0–1 probability** of high income.
- Has `predict_proba`, so ROC-AUC / PR-AUC are natural (linear regression needed a threshold wrapper + `decision_function`).
- Not on the required course list (linear/poly, Perceptron, SVM, k-NN, Tree, Naive Bayes), but kept for binary-classification comparison.

### 3. Settings

- `LogisticRegression(max_iter=1000, random_state=42)`
- `max_iter`: optimization step cap; default 100 can warn.
- Default preprocessing (`scale=True`, etc.). Same `make_model_pipeline` + `clone` pattern as other models.
