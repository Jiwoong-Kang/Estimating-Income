### 2. What `max_depth` is

- How many **levels of questions** the tree may ask.
- Small → simple (possible underfit) / large or `None` → deep (easy overfit)
- Candidates: `[3, 5, 8, 12, 15, 20, None]` (`None` = no depth limit)

### 5. Lessons from Naive Bayes tuning

- Knob: **`var_smoothing`** — floor added when variance is tiny
  - Small → barely added / large → smoother distributions
- Keep `dense=True` (GaussianNB cannot use sparse)
- Candidates: `[1e-11, 1e-9, 1e-7, 1e-5, 1e-3, 0.1]` (default `1e-9`)
- Independence issues may remain, but smoothing alone moves metrics a lot

### 8. Lessons from Logistic Regression tuning

- Knob: **`C`** — regularization strength (default L2). Same role as SVM’s `C`
  - Large `C` → weak regularization / small `C` → strong
- Fixed: `max_iter=1000`, `random_state=42`
- Candidates: `[0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100]`, select by CV **F1 mean**
