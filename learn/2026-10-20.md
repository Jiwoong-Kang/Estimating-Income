### 2. What SVM is

- Finds the separating boundary with the **largest margin**.
- No probabilities; uses `decision_function` (`evaluate.py` already handles this).

### 3. Kernel vs slack (easy to mix up)

| Idea | Role |
|---|---|
| **Kernel** (linear / RBF) | **Shape** of the boundary |
| **Slack (soft margin)** | Allow some mistakes / margin violations |

- RBF ≠ slack — different axes.
- `LinearSVC` is soft-margin by default; `C` controls how much violation is allowed.
- **RBF:** curved boundaries possible, but slow on many samples → OK to skip here.

### 4. Why `LinearSVC`

- Much **faster** than `SVC(kernel="linear")` on larger data.
- `dual=False`: recommended when n_samples >> n_features (Adult fits).
- `max_iter=5000`, `random_state=42`.

### 6. k-Nearest Neighbors (k-NN)

- Classify by majority vote of the **k nearest** neighbors (distance-based → **scaling matters**, keep `scale=True`).
- `n_neighbors=15`, `n_jobs=-1`. ~30k samples → CV is **slow**.
- Too small k: noise-sensitive; too large k: pulled toward majority (low income).

### 11. Naive Bayes (GaussianNB) and `dense`

- Assumes features are **independent** and multiplies probabilities.
- Cannot take sparse matrices → `OneHotEncoder(sparse_output=not dense)` + `make_model_pipeline(..., dense=True)`.
- EDA: strong relationship–sex / relationship–marital-status overlap → **independence often broken**.
